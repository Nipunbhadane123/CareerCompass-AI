# CareerCompass AI — User Guide & Feature Manual

## Welcome to CareerCompass AI
**Turn Your Skills Into Your Next Opportunity.**

CareerCompass AI guides you from an initial raw resume or course background to clear career choices, structured skill-gap analysis, job matching, and customized learning plans.

---

## 1. Getting Started: Registration & Onboarding
1. **Sign Up**: Navigate to `/register` and enter your name, email, and password.
2. **Onboarding Flow**:
   - **Step 1: Education & Experience**: Enter degree, college, graduation year, and professional experience.
   - **Step 2: Skills & Interests**: Select verified technical skills and personal interests.
   - **Step 3: Target Roles & Pace**: Pick your primary career target (e.g., "Full-Stack Engineer", "ML Engineer") and weekly study pace.
   - **Step 4: Resume Upload**: Optionally drag-and-drop your existing resume (PDF/DOCX) or proceed directly to the dashboard.

---

## 2. Using Resume Intelligence
1. Navigate to **Resume Intelligence** in the sidebar.
2. Upload your PDF or DOCX resume.
3. The platform processes the document through our extraction engine and displays:
   - **ATS Readability Score (0-100)**: Visualized as a circular gauge with breakdown.
   - **Extracted Profile**: Review education, experience timeline, projects, and detected skills.
   - **Strengths vs. Weaknesses**: Understand what shines and what missing elements trigger ATS filters.
   - **Actionable Suggestions**: Direct recommendations to enhance impact, metrics, and keyword alignment.
   - **Export Report**: Download or print a clean summary report.

---

## 3. Career Explorer & Skill-Gap Analysis
1. Navigate to **Career Explorer**.
2. Browse curated industry career paths across Software Engineering, Machine Learning, Cloud/DevOps, Data Science, and Product Management.
3. Click any role card to view:
   - Compatibility Match Score calculated against your verified skills.
   - Required vs. Preferred skills breakdown.
   - **Skill-Gap Diagnostic**: Green badges for acquired skills, amber/red badges for critical missing competencies.
   - Explainable fit analysis explaining the rationale behind your match.

---

## 4. Job Matching & Application Tracking
1. Navigate to **Job Matching**.
2. Explore curated sample vacancies or use the **Custom Job Analyzer**:
   - Paste any real job description from LinkedIn, Indeed, or company boards into the analyzer.
3. Review your compatibility score, missing keywords, and specific resume tailoring suggestions.
4. Save jobs to your personal tracker and update their status (`Saved`, `Applied`, `Interviewing`, `Offered`).

---

## 5. Personalized Learning Roadmaps
1. Navigate to **Learning Roadmap**.
2. Generate an actionable, sequenced learning roadmap for your target career role.
3. Follow ordered milestones designed with prerequisites in mind.
4. Access verified free learning resources (documentation, interactive tutorials, video courses) for each skill.
5. Check off completed milestones to watch your completion percentage progress in real-time.

---

## 6. Settings & Data Privacy
1. Navigate to **Settings** to update your profile preferences, target role, and study pace.
2. Switch AI provider settings (Gemini API or local deterministic engine).
3. Export your complete profile data or permanently delete your account and associated uploaded files with one click.
